Course 0: t27 basics in 27 lessons, each with an infographic

2026-10-07 · 5 min read

[the browser runner skips invariant and bench blocks; the lesson specs are not yet in gHashTag/t27] A new course comes before the FPGA and AI numbers courses: 27 lessons on the t27 language, each with a black-and-white table or diagram drawn from its own spec and a lesson spec that compiles on all 7 backends.

#t27#Course

The t27 courses started at the chip: course 1 teaches an FPGA and course 2 the numbers AI chips store. Both expect the reader to read a .t27 spec already. Course 0 comes before them and teaches that: 27 lessons on the language itself, in 9 modules of 3 lessons, ending where course 1 starts.

What each lesson opens

Every lesson opens one infographic and one spec. The infographic is a black-and-white table, and in 11 of the 27 lessons also a flow diagram; its words and rows live in a .t27 widget spec (specs/widgets/basics-<lesson>.t27), and a widget spec test checks that the table has whole rows. The lesson spec lives under specs/basics/ and compiles clean on all 7 backends in the browser, with its tests passing; the course generator refuses to build if one of them does not.

The tables are real HTML tables with a caption and header cells, and the diagrams are SVG with a title, so a screen reader reads both. On a phone the table turns into one card per row.

What it does not do yet

  • The browser runner skips invariant and bench blocks, so the lessons that show them describe them and do not run them.
  • A use line is checked by the compiler, but the browser does not load the module it names.
  • The lesson specs live in the site copy of the t27 spec tree; they are not yet in gHashTag/t27 itself.
