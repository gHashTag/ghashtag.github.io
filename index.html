The Two-Reading Principle

Every figure satisfies two readings simultaneously: a rigorous engineering blueprint fitting a 19th-century scientific atlas plate, and a fairy-tale azbuka page understandable to a child. Pure black ink on pure white #FFFFFF, three vertical panels, curly-fleuron bracket cartouches, hairline cross-hatching, tiny x/y/z axis arrows, serif-typeset φ formulae, and a TRINITY S³AI ribbon at the bottom. No human figures, no faces, no heraldry, no Cyrillic captions inside the artwork.
