What you get

Made for showing someone how to use an app.

A bubble that goes anywhere

Floats above every app and every Space, follows you into full screen. Drag it, scroll over it to resize. The record button is attached to it, so you can stop a take while another app has focus.

Four halos

None, a clean ring, a glow, or glass — which blurs and magnifies the screen behind the rim as real refraction, not a drawn-on highlight.

Auto-zoom that leads the click

Orb samples the pointer sixty times a second and pushes in toward what you clicked, arriving a beat before the click rather than chasing it. Amount, speed and hold are sliders you can move afterwards, or switch off entirely.

An editor for the take you already made

Reopen a finished recording, scrub to any moment, drag the bubble to where it should have been, change circle to square, then export.

The bubble is never in your screen track

Kept out twice over — the window is marked unshareable, and the capture filter names it as excluded. One stale flag should not be able to burn a Stop button into your finished video.
